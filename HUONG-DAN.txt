HUONG DAN UPLOAD - PROFILE TRUONG
=================================

Ban da gom day du giao dien hien tai, config, anh, nhac va loi bai hat.

1. Giai nen ZIP.
2. Upload TOAN BO noi dung sau khi giai nen vao thu muc web cua host
   (thuong la public_html, www hoac htdocs).
3. index.html phai nam ngay trong thu muc web, khong boc them thu muc con.
4. Giu nguyen ten file bai nhac va thu muc music/ de nhac va lyrics hoat dong.
5. Mo ten mien cua ban. Neu dang cap nhat ban cu, bam Ctrl + F5.

Day la website tinh: KHONG can npm install, npm start hay Node.js.
Host PHP co the su dung index.php; trang nay cung mo duoc bang index.html.

CHINH SUA CHO NGUOI KHAC
- Sua ten, avatar, Discord ID, lien ket, nhac va thoi tiet trong config.js.
- desktop.css / desktop.js: giao dien dong ho, cuon trang va tia sang.
- layout4.css / layout4.js: profile card va cac tien ich hien tai.
- music/lyrics1.js: loi bai hat dong bo.

CAN KET NOI INTERNET
Font, icon, avatar Discord, Lanyard va thoi tiet dang dung dich vu online.
Trinh duyet co the yeu cau bam vao trang truoc khi phat nhac.
Bam ten bai hat o phia tren de mo trinh phat nhac.

Khong gom .git, scraper Node.js, file nhac trung lap hay tai nguyen khong su dung.
